# Figure legends

Figures are in `figures/` (PNG 600 dpi + vector PDF). Regenerate with the scripts in `scripts/python/figures/`.

**Fig01_discovery_dmc.png** — Constitutive methylation divergence between Angus and Nellore (discovery, 108-gene panel, ±15 kb windows). (A) Positional distribution of the 160 significant DMCs (BH-FDR < 0.05, |Δβ| > 15%; 90 hypermethylated and 70 hypomethylated in the taurine background) across distal-upstream, proximal-promoter, gene-body, and distal-downstream windows. (B) Genes ranked by DMC count, coloured by module (heat-shock, tick-resistance, interferon-stimulated, milk-production, and other panel genes). (C) Leading principal component (PC1, 53.9% of variance) separating the five Angus from the five Nellore animals.

**Fig02_celltype_dmc.png** — Differentially methylated cytosines per contrast across the thirteen systematisation contrasts at the two effect-size thresholds, highlighting the subspecies-over-season gradient and the lymphoid-over-myeloid gradient across the seven immune cell types (C8–C14).

**Fig03_bridge_concordance.png** — Multi-omic bridge. Each point is a gene differentially methylated in the systematisation contrasts (x: regulatory Δβ, promoter/upstream when available) and differentially expressed under heat stress (y: log2 fold-change). Green: canonical repressive concordance; point size ∝ priority score; dark outline ring: gene carries a promoter DMC.

**Fig04_module_enrichment.png** — Functional-module representation among the concordant genes (hypergeometric test with the curated 108-gene panel as the universe). The thermotolerance/heat-shock module carries the most concordant genes (5/21, p = 0.29) and the unfolded-protein-response module the lowest nominal p (2/5, p = 0.21), with the interferon-stimulated-gene module at 2/7 (p = 0.36). Because the universe is the curated candidate panel rather than a genome-wide background, these are within-panel tendencies and not genome-wide enrichments; no module survives Benjamini–Hochberg (smallest adjusted q ≈ 0.65), and among the interferon members only *ISG15* is supported by multiple concordant DMCs.

**Fig05_C6_validation.png** — Orthogonal validation in fetal-liver WGBS (MacPhillamy et al. 2024). Of 47 bridge candidates, 25 carry a fetal-liver DMR and 15 in the same taurine-versus-indicine direction; the somatotropic/heat-shock candidates (*GHSR*, *HSPA2*, *PRLR*) are corroborated in the same direction across assay and tissue, while *ISG15* is blood/immune-restricted (no fetal-liver DMR).

**Fig06_matched_atlas.png** — Matched-sample validation in a taurine WGBS+RNA-seq tissue atlas (Zhou et al. 2020; one Holstein cow, ten tissues). Distribution across the crossed panel genes of the Spearman correlation between promoter methylation and expression; 61 of 88 genes (69%; *HPRT1* not testable) show the expected negative (repressive) correlation. Vertical lines mark selected candidate genes (*ISG15* strongest at r = −0.67, p = 0.033); no correlation survives Benjamini–Hochberg correction and the strongest repressive values include non-prioritized controls, so the atlas supports a panel-level repressive direction without validating any individual candidate. The cis-genetic classification of the 2719 subspecies DMCs against the 1000 Bull Genomes catalogue and the Gyr-versus-Holstein genomic differentiation of the candidates (Section 2.12) are provided in Online Resources 6 and 7.
